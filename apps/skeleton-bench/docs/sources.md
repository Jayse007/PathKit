| pump-casing | Casing | Job: houses the impeller, holds the pumped liquid and guides the flow from the impeller to the discharge; its spiral (volute) shape turns the liquid's speed into pressure. Material: cast iron is one common casing material; ductile iron and stainless steel (SS316) are also used. Position: surrounds the impeller at the wet end of the pump. | https://www.slurrypumpsupply.com/news/centrifugal-pump-parts-guide.html | TO CONFIRM |
| pump-impeller | Impeller | Job: the rotating part with curved vanes that throws the liquid outward and gives it speed and pressure; it has the largest effect on flow, head and efficiency. Material: cast iron for clean water; stainless steel (for example CF8M) for corrosive liquids. Position: inside the casing, on the end of the shaft. | https://www.slurrypumpsupply.com/news/centrifugal-pump-parts-guide.html | TO CONFIRM |
| pump-shaft | Shaft | Job: carries the turning force from the motor to the impeller and holds the impeller in place. Material: alloy steel such as AISI 4140 for general duty; 431 or duplex stainless steel where corrosion resistance is needed. Position: runs from the drive end, through the bearing housing and seal, to the impeller in the casing. | https://www.slurrypumpsupply.com/news/centrifugal-pump-parts-guide.html | TO CONFIRM |
| pump-bearing-housing | Bearing housing | Job: holds the bearings that support the shaft and carry its radial and axial (thrust) loads, keeping the shaft in line. Material: the bearings are typically chrome steel (AISI 52100) or bronze sleeve bearings; the housing material is not stated by these sources, so the text names the bearings only. Position: between the motor side and the wet end, around the shaft. | https://www.slurrypumpsupply.com/news/centrifugal-pump-parts-guide.html | TO CONFIRM |
| pump-mechanical-seal | Mechanical seal | Job: stops the pumped liquid leaking out along the turning shaft. Two flat faces, one turning with the shaft and one fixed to the casing, are pressed together by springs, with a thin liquid film between them. Material: faces of carbon, silicon carbide or tungsten carbide; rubber-like secondary seals (for example Viton or EPDM). Position: where the shaft passes through the casing, in the seal chamber (stuffing box) area. | https://www.slurrypumpsupply.com/news/centrifugal-pump-parts-guide.html | TO CONFIRM |
| pump-flange | Flange | Job: the pump has a suction flange that takes liquid in and a discharge flange that sends the pressurised liquid on; the flanges connect the pump to the pipework. Material: the flanges are part of the pump body, for example cast iron with ANSI 125 or PN16 flanges. In an end-suction pump the liquid enters at the end of the casing and leaves at the top. Position: suction at the end of the casing, discharge on top. | https://www.slurrypumpsupply.com/news/centrifugal-pump-parts-guide.html (suction and discharge flange) ; https://kendisco.com/blog/end-suction-pump/ (inlet at the end, outlet at the top) ; https://www.pentair.com/content/dam/extranet/web/nam/aurora/brochures/A-02-10581.pdf (cast iron body with ANSI-125/PN16 flanges) | TO CONFIRM |
